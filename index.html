Structured Music Curriculum

Music Classes — Online & Offline

Join our progressive grade-based learning paths tailored for beginners, church worship team members, and aspiring professionals.

Online + Offline 12 Weeks • Grade 1-4

Contemporary Worship Keyboard

Chord inversions, worship pads, ambient synth textures, transposing on the fly, and leading congregational worship from keys.

Instructors: Ps. Robin Moses & Bro. Allan Benny
Online + Offline 10 Weeks • All Levels

Acoustic & Electric Guitar

Strumming dynamics, open/suspended worship chords, fingerpicking patterns, capoing techniques, scale theory, and solo lead fills.

Instructors: Bro. Sarvanan & Bro. Allan Benny
Offline Bangalore Studio 8 Weeks • Intensive

Drummers for the Sanctuary

Metronome locking, dynamic control for church sanctuary environments, 6/8 ballad grooves, syncopation, and smooth song transitions.

Instructor: Bro. Emmanuel
Online + Offline 8 Weeks • All Vocalists

Worship Vocal Coaching & Harmony

Breath support, pitch accuracy, vocal agility, blending 3-part backing vocals (Soprano, Alto, Tenor), and worship ministry stamina.

Instructor: Sis. Aruna Prasad

Rev. Dr. Sunny Prasad
Heart for the Local Church

Equipping Generations to Lead Spirit & Truth Worship

Rev. Dr. Sunny Prasad is the president of Enrich Worship Music Academy, a ministry dedicated to catering to the vital needs of the local church in the areas of biblical worship, musician development, and discipleship.

He serves as the Youth and Worship Coordinator for the Central District Council of South India Assemblies of God (CDSIAG Karnataka & Goa).

Dr. Prasad actively teaches Worship Leadership across prominent Bible colleges, authors essential chord handbooks, and travels extensively conducting practical worship team bootcamps.
